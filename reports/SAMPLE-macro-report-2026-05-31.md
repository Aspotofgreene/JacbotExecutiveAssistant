## 1. Inflation
**Current status:** U.S. inflation around 3.8% YoY, with energy the largest
contributor. Canada around 2.4%, above the BoC's 2% target but relatively controlled.
**Interpretation:** Inflation is no longer falling smoothly; oil and geopolitics are
creating renewed pressure, making central banks more cautious about cuts.
**Market impact:** Negative for high-growth tech if inflation keeps rising; positive
for energy; mixed for banks.
**Watch level:** 🟡 Moderate concern

## 2. Unemployment
**Current status:** U.S. ~4.3%. Canada rose to ~6.9%.
**Interpretation:** U.S. labor market still relatively strong; Canada weakening and
showing more economic stress.
**Market impact:** Strong employment supports consumer spending; weakening employment
increases recession risk.
**Watch level:** 🟡 Mild concern

## 3. Central Bank Policy
**Current status:** Fed ~3.5–3.75%, with markets sharply trimming near-term cut
expectations. BoC ~2.25%, officials wary of labor-market weakness and inflation
uncertainty.
**Interpretation:** Both remain restrictive; "higher-for-longer" is still the theme.
**Market impact:** Negative for speculative growth; positive for cash and fixed-income
yields; mixed for equities.
**Watch level:** 🟡 Moderate concern

## 4. Earnings Growth
**Current status:** Large-cap tech / AI names keep producing strong earnings, the
primary support for equities. Strong: AI, semiconductors, cloud, digital platforms.
Weak: cyclical manufacturing, interest-sensitive industries.
**Interpretation:** Earnings still justify elevated valuations; leadership remains
concentrated.
**Market impact:** Very important for QQQ and S&P 500 ETFs.
**Watch level:** 🟢 Positive

## 5. Bond Yields
**Current status:** U.S. 10-year Treasury recently approached ~4.6%; long-end yields
elevated.
**Interpretation:** The bond market is signaling inflation concern, heavy government
borrowing, and higher long-term rates — one of the most important things to watch.
**Market impact:** Negative for expensive growth; pressure on housing/borrowing;
attractive entry for future bond buyers.
**Watch level:** 🟠 Elevated concern

## 6. Oil Prices
**Current status:** Highly volatile on Iran-related tensions; Brent recently fell below
$100 after peace talks but stays elevated vs history.
**Interpretation:** Currently one of the most important macro variables — high oil
lifts inflation and transport costs and slows growth.
**Market impact:** Positive for Canadian energy and the TSX; negative for consumers,
airlines, manufacturing, and the inflation outlook.
**Watch level:** 🟠 Elevated concern

## 7. Valuation Metrics
**Current status:** S&P 500 expensive vs history; QQQ very expensive with heavy AI
optimism priced in; Canadian market more reasonable.
**Interpretation:** The market leans hard on continued earnings growth and the AI
capex cycle. Even excellent companies can be poor investments at excessive prices.
**Market impact:** U.S. growth vulnerable to disappointment; Canada relatively cheaper.
**Watch level:** 🟡 Moderate concern

## 8. Recession Probability
**Current status:** U.S. — no confirmed recession, moderate risk. Canada — two
consecutive quarters of GDP contraction.
**Interpretation:** Canada technically meets a common recession definition, though
conditions are mixed (employment functioning, consumers spending vs GDP contraction,
weak investment, deteriorating labor market).
**Watch level:** 🟠 Elevated concern (Canada) / 🟡 Moderate concern (U.S.)
